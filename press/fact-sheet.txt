One-line description:
An offline Android obstacle-course game where a hippo steers a circular raft with fart-powered thrust.

How it plays:
- The on-screen joystick positions your hippo on the raft's rim. Hold FART and the raft is pushed the opposite way.
- There is no brake button. Move around the rim and fart against your momentum, or let the raft coast.
- Holding FART drains a gas tank. Release to coast and refill, or collect beans along the course to top it up.
- 100 courses with turns, ramps, jumps, rushing water, bouncing edges and moving hazards, plus checkpoints.
- Coins earned from adventure progress unlock outfits, hats, body colours and puff effects. Cosmetics never change the raft's physics.
