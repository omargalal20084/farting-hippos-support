Hippos Local Publisher Tools: privacy policy

Effective November 18, 2026. This policy covers Brilliant Thoughts' private Windows publisher utility, not the Farting Hippos game.

Google access and purpose

The utility requests basic Google email identity to confirm the authorized publisher account, and the Gmail send permission to send publisher-authorized messages. It does not request permission to read emails, replies, contacts, or attachments. A separate service account downloads read-only, aggregated Google Play reports for Farting Hippos to evaluate its performance.

Local storage and disclosure

OAuth refresh credentials and the service-account key are encrypted on the publisher's Windows computer, outside the game project, with access restricted to the Windows user and SYSTEM. Reports, prepared outgoing messages, and campaign records are stored locally. A local send ledger records message identifiers, recipients, timestamps, content hashes, and delivery-request status to prevent duplicate sends.

Google processes authentication requests and outgoing messages; addressed recipients receive those messages. Credentials are not included in public assets or supplied to AI models. The utility does not sell Google user data, use it for advertising targeting, or use it to train generalized AI models.

Hippos Local Publisher Tools' use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Retention and control

The publisher retains local records until they are deleted. Access can be revoked through the Google Account's third-party connections page and the Play Console's user permissions. Revocation prevents subsequent authorized access; it does not erase existing local records or messages already delivered. Local credentials and records can be deleted separately by the publisher.
