Change Gravity

Privacy policy and support
Last updated: 24 October 2026

The rebuilt Change Gravity release is an offline, single-player game. It has no advertisements, analytics, accounts or real-money purchases. Game progress is stored on your device.

Scope and older versions

This policy covers the rebuilt, ad-free Change Gravity release by Brilliant Thoughts, with package name com.BrilliantThoughts.ChangeGravity.

Retired versions, including version 1.42, contained Google Mobile Ads and Facebook integrations configured for advertising identifiers and automatic app events. Those versions are not described by the rebuilt release's no-ads/no-analytics behavior. Publishing this policy alone does not disable code in older installations. The rebuilt release removes those integrations.

Information stored locally

The rebuilt game stores level unlocks, stars earned per level and in total, cosmetic unlocks and selections, new-item indicators, music and sound-effect settings, tutorial preferences, zoom and orientation preferences. These are local game records, not an online account or public profile.

Cosmetic unlocks are part of gameplay. The rebuilt release does not use real-money purchases or an online store.

Collection and sharing

The rebuilt game does not send personal information or gameplay records to Brilliant Thoughts, advertising networks or analytics providers. It has no retained advertising, analytics, cloud diagnostics, account sign-in, gameplay upload or online multiplayer service.

The rebuilt game does not access your location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera. If you explicitly open its privacy link, the link is opened by your device's browser rather than an in-game account service. No player profile or gameplay values are attached to that policy link.

Children and families

The rebuilt game is intended for players aged six and older, including children. Its official content rating is determined separately by rating authorities. The rebuilt release has no advertisements, public player profiles, chat or purchases. Parents and guardians can contact us with questions about privacy.

Retention and deletion

Your progress and settings remain on your device until they are cleared. Android's app-storage controls can delete local game data, including progress, stars and cosmetic selections. We do not operate a cloud-save service or hold a copy of those records. Any device-level backup or transfer provided by your platform is governed by that platform's settings and privacy policy.

Support correspondence

If you choose to email us, we receive your email address and the information in your message. We use it to answer your request, investigate an issue or address a privacy concern. We retain correspondence only as needed for that purpose and applicable legal obligations. You can contact us to request deletion. Please do not send passwords, payment details or unnecessary personal information.

Platform services and this website

Google Play and Android provide installation and other platform services under Google's Privacy Policy.

This page is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process request information, including IP addresses, to operate and protect the hosting service. We have not added advertisements, analytics scripts or tracking cookies to this page. See GitHub's Privacy Statement.
