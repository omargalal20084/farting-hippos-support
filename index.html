Privacy policy and support
Last updated: 23 October 2026

The current release is a single-player game. No account is required. The game has no advertising or real-money purchases, and does not send gameplay profiles or personal information to Brilliant Thoughts.

About this policy

This policy applies to Farting Hippos, published by Brilliant Thoughts on Google Play with package name com.brilliantthoughts.fartinghippos.

Information stored on your device

The game saves level progress, best results, earned coins, cosmetic purchases and equipment, trophy progress, and game preferences locally on your device. Coins are earned through gameplay and are not real money. We do not operate a server that receives these game profiles.

Your device may include app data in backups according to your Android and Google account settings. These platform backups are controlled by you and your platform provider, not by Brilliant Thoughts.

Accounts, advertising and analytics

The current release does not require registration or sign-in. It has no ads, advertising identifiers, enabled analytics service, or enabled cloud crash reporting. It does not access your contacts, camera, microphone, photos or precise location.

Multiplayer is unavailable in this release. A future version that adds online features or changes how information is handled will have an updated privacy policy and the appropriate disclosures before those features become available.

Children and families

The game is intended for players aged six and older, including children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through the game. The current version has no chat, public profiles, advertisements or real-money purchases. Parents or guardians can contact us with privacy concerns.

Support messages

If you choose to email us, we receive the email address and information you include in your message. We use that correspondence to answer your request, troubleshoot issues or address a privacy concern. Please do not send passwords, payment details, or unnecessary personal information. Support correspondence is kept only as needed to handle the request and meet applicable legal obligations.

Deleting information

You can remove locally saved progress using Android's app-storage controls. Clearing storage deletes your local coins, cosmetics, records and trophies. Manage any platform backups separately in your device or Google account settings. To request deletion of support correspondence, contact us using the address below.

Platform services and this website

Google Play and Android provide download, installation, security and other platform services under their own policies. See Google's Privacy Policy.

This privacy/support website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process basic request information, such as IP addresses, to operate and secure its hosting service. We have not added advertising, analytics scripts or tracking cookies to this page. See GitHub's Privacy Statement.
