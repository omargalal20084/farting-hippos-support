Chickens On A Mission

Privacy policy and support
Last updated: 24 October 2026

The rebuilt game is an offline, single-player game with no advertisements, analytics, accounts or real-money purchases. Your scores and nicknames stay on your device.

Scope

This policy covers the rebuilt Chickens On A Mission app by Brilliant Thoughts, with package name com.brilliantthoughts.chickensonamission.

It does not describe the retired app with package name brilliantThoughts.omar.chickensonamission. That older app included advertising and analytics software. The rebuilt app is a separate installation; this policy does not retroactively change how the older app operates.

What the game stores

The game stores music and sound-effect preferences, and up to ten local high-score entries containing a nickname, score and accuracy rating. The nickname prompt asks for a made-up nickname; a real name is not needed.

Temporary paused-game state and an unfinished nickname entry may be retained by Android so the game can restore its screen after an interruption. These values are not sent to Brilliant Thoughts or an online leaderboard.

Collection and sharing

The rebuilt game does not collect or share user data with Brilliant Thoughts or third-party advertising or analytics services. It has no advertising, analytics SDK, account sign-in, online leaderboard, WebView or in-app purchases, and does not request access to your location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera.

The Android app does not request Internet permission. If you explicitly tap the privacy link, Android opens this page in an external browser. The game does not attach your nickname, scores, preferences or device identifiers to that link.

Children and families

The rebuilt game is intended for older children, teenagers and adults. Follow the content rating shown in your region; the North American ESRB rating is Everyone 10+ for fantasy violence. It has no chat, public player profiles, advertisements or purchases. Parents and guardians can contact us with privacy concerns.

Retention and deletion

Game records and preferences remain locally until you clear app storage or uninstall the game. Clearing storage deletes the saved nicknames, scores and preferences. Android app-data backup is disabled for this rebuilt app; we do not operate a cloud-save service or hold a copy of your game records.

Support correspondence

If you choose to email us, we receive your email address and the information you include. We use that correspondence to answer your request or investigate an issue, and keep it only as needed to handle the request and meet applicable legal obligations. You can request deletion of support correspondence by contacting us. Please do not send passwords, payment details or unnecessary personal information.

Platform services and this website

Google Play and Android provide installation and other platform services under Google's Privacy Policy.

This page is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process request information, including IP addresses, to operate and protect its hosting service. We have not added advertisements, analytics scripts or tracking cookies to this page. See GitHub's Privacy Statement.
